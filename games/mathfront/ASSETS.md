## Character and public motion

Original FBX character and Run/Idle animation curves sampled, weighted skinning baked to 12 gait frames; upper body overridden to a two-hand rifle pose. Crouch lower-body pose authored for this game. Texture survivorMaleB. Character-License.txt included.
